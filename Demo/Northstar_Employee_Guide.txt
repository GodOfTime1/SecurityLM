NORTHSTAR ROBOTICS - EMPLOYEE QUICK GUIDE
Version 1.0 | Fictional hackathon test document

All company names, procedures, and policies below are invented for testing.
This document contains no real employee records, passwords, or API keys.

VPN CONNECTION
To connect to the company VPN:
1. Open the Northstar Connect application.
2. Select the Northstar-Staff connection profile.
3. Sign in with your company account and complete multifactor authentication.
4. Wait until the status reads Connected before opening internal applications.

VPN ERROR 42
VPN error 42 means the connection profile is out of date.
Disconnect, open Settings, select Refresh Connection Profile, and reconnect.
If error 42 continues, create an IT support ticket. Include the error number,
your operating system, and when the problem started. Never include a password
or an authentication code in a ticket.

SUPPORT HOURS
The IT help desk is available Monday through Friday, from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM
Eastern Time. Routine support tickets have a target first response of one
business day. This is a response target, not a guaranteed resolution time.

LOST COMPANY LAPTOP
Report a lost company laptop to the IT help desk immediately. Include its
asset tag if known. IT will begin the account-protection and device-locking
process. Do not wait until the next weekly team meeting to report the loss.

REMOTE WORK
Employees may work remotely up to two days per week with manager approval.
Use a company-managed device, connect through the VPN for internal services,
and avoid discussing confidential information in public spaces.

EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT
Submit work-related expenses within 30 calendar days of purchase. Attach an
itemized receipt and a short business justification. Expenses over $250
require manager approval before purchase.

DOCUMENT AND AI USAGE
Use only approved company AI tools for internal documents. Do not submit
customer information, credentials, or restricted documents to a public AI
service. An AI-generated answer is assistance, not authorization to bypass
company policy. Ask the appropriate team when a policy is unclear.

END OF FICTIONAL GUIDE
